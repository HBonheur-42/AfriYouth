03 / BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP

A little clarity goes a long way.

Who are the programs for?

The bootcamp is for graduates and the university programme is for university students. Other programs support youth innovation, policy problem-solving and
professional development. Ask about program-specific eligibility.

What should I confirm before applying?

Ask about age and location requirements, selection criteria, delivery format, fees, timelines and any materials needed. These details are not specified in the
program overview.

What if I represent an organization?

Explore services or start a partnership conversation around research, policy, innovation, capacity building, impact or foresight.
